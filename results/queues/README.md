## The queues

| Queue | Task instances | What the reviewer must do |
|---|---|---|
| `ta_adjudication.csv` | **194** | Read the title and abstract. Record your own decision, independent of the suggestion shown. Then fill decision, decision_basis=independently_adjudicated, reason_code (if excluding), reviewer, screened_date and adjudicated_date in data/screening/screening_ta.csv. |

| `ft_assessment.csv` | **0** | Assess the retrieved full text against the eligibility criteria. Record decision, decision_basis, reason_code (if excluding), reviewer, screened_date and adjudicated_date in data/screening/screening_ft.csv. |

## How the queues overlap

The queues are different **tasks**, some of them on the same **records**, which is why the task-instance total exceeds the number of distinct reports.

- Every verification record is drawn from the title/abstract population, so the verification queue is contained in it.
- Every full-text retrieval record is a title/abstract **include**, so the retrieval queue is contained in that subset.
- A record can therefore appear in both the verification and the retrieval queue, and some do.
- The union of all queues is **194 distinct reports**.

## Dependencies between the queues

`ta_adjudication` gates everything downstream: until a report's title/abstract decision is the reviewer's own, the full-text row it produced is provisional and the formal PRISMA flow counts it nowhere. `blinded_verification` is independent of the others and can be done at any time; it measures one reviewer's blinded judgement against the automated suggestion set, and is **not** duplicate screening. `ft_assessment` cannot begin for a report until its full text is recorded as retrieved.
